Financial ML 2025 — 2026 Solo project

Islamic Bank Risk Analysis Platform

Uzbekistan approved its Islamic banking law in 2026; this is the credit-risk infrastructure a bank entering that market needs on day one. An ensemble of logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM produces PD, LGD and expected loss for every application, with a SHAP explanation attached to each decision. On top of the model sit a Sharia compliance engine covering eight AAOIFI contract types, AML/FATF pattern scoring, IFRS 9 stage classification, and an immutable audit log. Portfolio risk is measured with Vasicek ASRF simulation, Monte Carlo stress tests, and VaR/CVaR at 95%.

Computer vision 2026 Solo project

SafeVision AI — hard-hat compliance monitoring

Construction-site cameras, checked in real time for missing helmets, with a Telegram alert when a violation holds. A single detector kept calling caps, hair and raised hands "helmet", so the pipeline became two stages: YOLO11m locates heads, then a YOLO11s classifier crops each head and answers one question — is that an actual construction helmet? The second stage costs 0.1 ms and removed the false positives the detector alone could not.

Bukhara Regional AI Assistant

Ask a regional statistics database a question in plain Uzbek and get back an answer, a table, or a full report. Geospatial ML 2026 TenzorSoft

Agro AI — field health from orbit

Draw a field boundary and the service pulls its Sentinel history from Google Earth Engine, builds NDVI and NDMI time series, masks the raster to the exact polygon, and reads the seasonal curve: bare soil, winter cover, or a crop at peak. A model then turns the numbers into an agronomist's recommendation — likely crop, drought and infection risk, fertiliser need. Built over district boundaries for the Tashkent, Bukhara and Qashqadaryo regions, with an STRtree index so parcel lookups stay fast at scale.

Voice agents 2026 Solo project

Secretar AI — an Uzbek voice assistant that stays on your machine

A tray-resident assistant with the whole pipeline under your own control: an offline Vosk wake word, a GPU Uzbek Whisper fine-tune for speech-to-text, your own LLM over Ollama with native tool calling, and Uzbek XTTS speech played batch by batch so the reply starts before it has finished generating. Multi-step requests hand off to a planner → executor → error-handler loop that runs in the background. Safety is enforced in code, not requested in the prompt: destructive tools stay locked until the user actually confirms, the filesystem is an allowlist that refuses credential files outright, and untrusted content — PDF text, OCR, scraped pages — is fenced as data and blocks destructive tools for the rest of the turn.
